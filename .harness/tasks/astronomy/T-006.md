## Context

002 §16 and ADR-008 require transformations to be isolated rather than smeared through each planet model. The answered questions fix all three V1 frames as mean equinox and ecliptic of date; J2000-suffixed frames are explicitly future work. Geocentric states are derived by vector subtraction of Earth's heliocentric state (001 §16), which this module provides as a primitive but does not itself orchestrate.

## Task

Implement packages/astronomy/src/coordinates: the spherical/Cartesian conversions for both the ecliptic and equatorial systems, the ecliptic↔equatorial rotation driven by an epoch-dependent obliquity, and the heliocentric→geocentric translation primitive. Document each transformation's source frame, destination frame, units and conventions. Keep the module free of body-specific knowledge.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Conversions implemented and tested: ecliptic Cartesian ↔ ecliptic spherical, equatorial Cartesian ↔ equatorial spherical, ecliptic Cartesian ↔ equatorial Cartesian
- [ ] Axis conventions are documented in code exactly as 002 §8 states (x toward reference longitude zero, y toward +90° ecliptic longitude, z north of the ecliptic) and asserted by unit tests on canonical unit vectors
- [ ] The obliquity of the ecliptic is computed from an explicit epoch/time argument using a cited formula, is never a hard-coded constant inside a body model, and its source and validity range are documented
- [ ] Every conversion function names its source and destination frame in its signature or its name; no function converts between unspecified frames
- [ ] Round-trip property tests (cartesian → spherical → cartesian and ecliptic → equatorial → ecliptic) pass to documented tolerances
- [ ] Pole and near-zero cases are tested: latitude ±90°, vectors on the x-axis, near-zero-magnitude vectors, and longitude wraparound at 0/2π
- [ ] A frame-translation helper produces geocentric vectors from heliocentric ones by subtraction (target − Earth) and is tested independently of any body model
- [ ] All V1 frames are documented as mean equinox and ecliptic of date
- [ ] No body-specific or model-specific logic appears in this module
